# Affordability and Debt Service Assessment

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 2.1 Total debt servicing ratio for individuals {#2.1}

All individual borrowers are subject to a total debt servicing ratio (TDSR)
cap of 55% of gross monthly qualifying income. TDSR aggregates the proposed
instalment, all existing obligations from the bureau report, and 3% of
outstanding unsecured revolving balances treated as a monthly commitment.
Joint borrowers use combined qualifying income after applying the income
haircuts in section 1.3 of POL-CR-001. Guarantor obligations at 50% weight
must be included where the guaranteed facility is delinquent. Breach of the
55% cap is a hard decline and cannot be cured by exception.

## 2.2 Debt service coverage ratio for business borrowers {#2.2}

Small-business term facilities require a debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)
of at least 1.25x, computed as adjusted EBITDA divided by total annual debt
service including the proposed facility. Secured facilities with qualifying
collateral may use a 1.15x floor. DSCR between 1.0x and the applicable floor
may proceed only as a 12-month reviewable facility with quarterly covenant
testing. DSCR below 1.0x is a decline. Add-backs to EBITDA are limited to
documented owner compensation in excess of market rate and verified
non-recurring expenses under 10% of revenue.

## 2.3 Stressed interest rate {#2.3}

Affordability must be tested at the higher of the contractual rate plus
200 basis points or a floor rate of 5.5% per annum. Floating-rate facilities
use the 3-month compounded benchmark average plus contractual spread, then
add the 200 basis point stress. Fixed-rate facilities with tenure under
3 years re-test at the prevailing floating reference rate plus stress at
each repricing. Property-backed exposures additionally apply a 30% rental
void haircut in the stress scenario. The stressed instalment, not the
contractual one, feeds TDSR and DSCR.

## 2.4 Variable and non-standard income treatment {#2.4}

Commission, freelance, gig-economy, and bonus income is averaged over
24 months and haircut by 30% before inclusion. Applicants with less than
24 months of variable-income history use 50% recognition. Project-based
income recognised on completion certificates is capped at 60% of the
2-year average. Foreign-currency income is converted at the 12-month
average spot rate less a 10% adverse-movement haircut. Income from more
than 2 concurrent employers requires 12 months of combined continuity
before any of it qualifies.

## 2.5 Affordability review triggers {#2.5}

A fresh affordability assessment is mandatory on limit increases, tenure
extensions, Top-up applications, and any restructuring. Material adverse
change in bureau status (new delinquency, score drop exceeding 80 points)
triggers re-assessment within 30 days for facilities above S$100,000.
Annual review applies to all revolving facilities above S$150,000 and all
business facilities regardless of size. Where re-assessment breaches TDSR
or DSCR, the limit must be reduced to the compliant level within 60 days
unless a time-bound remediation plan is approved under POL-CR-005.
