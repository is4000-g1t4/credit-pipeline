# Servicing Collections and Hardship

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 7.1 Delinquency buckets and contact rules {#7.1}

Accounts are bucketed at 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ days past due (DPD),
with a dedicated handler assigned from 31 DPD. Contact is limited to 2
attempts per day and only between 9am and 9pm; workplace contact is allowed
only after 2 failed home-contact attempts. Promises to pay are recorded
with amount and date; 2 broken promises escalate the account to the next
bucket strategy immediately. Field visits commence at 61 DPD for balances
above S$20,000. All contact attempts and outcomes are logged the same day.

## 7.2 Hardship assistance {#7.2}

Borrowers facing illness, job loss, or natural disaster may apply for
hardship assistance once in any 12-month period. Approved hardship grants a
payment deferral of up to 3 months with interest capitalised, after which
the tenure extends by the deferred period. Applicants must show the hardship
is temporary with evidence of resumed repayment capacity within 6 months.
Hardship is unavailable for investment-property loans, for borrowers already
in a debt-consolidation programme, or where fraud is suspected. A second
hardship request within 12 months needs committee approval and a 20%
lump-sum good-faith payment.

## 7.3 Restructuring standards {#7.3}

Restructures are permitted only where post-restructure DSCR reaches at
least 1.0x for businesses or TDSR returns within 55% for individuals,
verified on fresh documents. Principal haircuts are prohibited; relief is
limited to tenure extension (maximum 5 additional years), temporary rate
reduction (maximum 24 months), and capitalisation of arrears up to 3
instalments. Each borrower is allowed one restructure per 24 months; a
second restructure requires committee approval and additional collateral or
guarantee enhancement. Restructured accounts are flagged and monitored
monthly for 12 months.

## 7.4 Write-off and recovery {#7.4}

Unsecured balances at 180 DPD with no payment in 90 days are recommended
for write-off; secured balances at 270 DPD with collateral shortfall above
30% follow the same path. Write-off requires dual approval at manager level
and above, with fraud cases referred to legal before any write-off.
Post-write-off recovery continues for 6 years via agency assignment with
commission capped at 25% of recoveries. Settlement discounts up to 30% are
permitted for lump-sum closure of written-off accounts; anything deeper
needs committee sign-off.

## 7.5 Complaints and dispute handling {#7.5}

Collection-related complaints must be acknowledged within 2 business days
and resolved within 21 days, with the account placed on hold from further
escalation during investigation. Disputed fees or charges above S$500 are
reviewed by an officer independent of the collections team. Findings adverse
to the bank result in fee reversal plus a goodwill review capped at S$1,000
without committee involvement. All upheld complaints feed the quarterly
conduct dashboard and the responsible unit's quality score.
