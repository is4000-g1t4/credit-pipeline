# Decision Engine Credit Standard

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 9.0 Scope and precedence {#9.0}

This standard records the automatic-processing bands and approval authorities
applied by the credit decision engine for individual and SME lending. It
supplements POL-CR-001, POL-CR-002, POL-CR-003, POL-CR-004 and POL-CR-005.
Where this standard and another credit policy differ, the stricter
eligibility requirement applies. POL-CR-005 governs whether a deviation from
this standard is permitted. This standard does not replace documentation,
pricing, servicing, or affordability remedies in those policies.

## 9.1 Individual automatic-processing bands {#9.1}

Individual unsecured applications are processed between S$1,000 and
S$25,000 for a term of 12 to 60 months. The total debt servicing ratio is
automatically processed at or below 35%, manually reviewed above 35% and up
to 50%, and declined above 50%. The credit score is automatically processed
at 700 or above, manually reviewed from 580 to 699, and declined below 580.
The automatic-processing amount is at most S$15,000. Employment of at least
1 year is required for automatic processing; shorter employment is manually
reviewed. Amounts or terms outside these product bounds are outside the
product and are declined.

## 9.2 SME automatic-processing bands {#9.2}

SME term applications are processed between S$5,000 and S$100,000 for a term
of 12 to 60 months. Annual revenue below S$50,000 is a decline; this is the
engine floor and does not replace the S$200,000 eligibility gate in POL-CR-003
section 3.1. Trading history below 1 year is a decline, 1 year up to but not
including 2 years is manually reviewed, and 2 years or more is eligible for
automatic processing. The debt service coverage ratio is automatically
processed at 1.5x or above, manually reviewed from 1.0x up to but not
including 1.5x, and declined below 1.0x. The credit score bands are the same
as section 9.1. The automatic-processing amount is at most S$25,000.
Hospitality and construction sectors trigger a sector review.

## 9.3 Approval authorities {#9.3}

Facilities up to S$50,000 may be approved by a single credit officer. Amounts
above S$50,000 up to S$200,000 require a team manager countersignature.
Anything above S$200,000, any staff application, any politically exposed
person, any connected-party application, an officer's own application, or an
override of a system decline requires the credit committee with a minimum
quorum of 3 voting members. These authorities apply to individual and SME
facilities.

## 9.4 Exception applicability {#9.4}

The exception taxonomy in POL-CR-005 governs this standard. No exception is
permitted for a credit score below 580, a debt service coverage ratio below
1.0x, trading history below 1 year, a total debt servicing ratio above 55%,
or an amount or term outside the product bounds. Only the exception states
recorded in the policy decision registry may be excepted under POL-CR-005
section 5.1; refer triggers, informational triggers, and structural remedies
are not exceptions. Where POL-CR-002 section 2.2 provides a 12-month
reviewable facility for a debt service coverage ratio between 1.0x and the
applicable floor, that structural remedy is used instead of an exception.
Where POL-CR-003 section 3.1 permits a secured facility for a business with
12 to 24 months of trading, that secured route is a structural remedy, not an
exception. A case with 2 or more exception-eligible rule breaches requires
credit committee approval regardless of amount, as stated in POL-CR-005
section 5.1.
