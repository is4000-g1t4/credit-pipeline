# Policy Exceptions and Overrides

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 5.1 Exception limits and dual control {#5.1}

Exceptions may not exceed 10% of monthly approvals by value in any business
unit; breach of the cap freezes all further exceptions until month-end
review. Every exception requires dual sign-off at least one level above the
standard approval authority for that amount. The exception memo must state
the breached rule, compensating factors, and a time-bound regularisation
plan not exceeding 12 months. Single-rule breaches only: cases breaching
2 or more policy rules require credit committee approval regardless of
amount.

## 5.2 Prohibited exceptions {#5.2}

No exception is permitted for sanctions or anti-money-laundering screening
hits, falsified or forged documents, applicant misrepresentation, bureau
scores below 580, TDSR breaches above 55%, or DSCR below 1.0x. Excluded
sectors under POL-CR-003 section 3.1 can never be onboarded by exception.
Any attempt to re-route a prohibited exception as a pricing adjustment or
restructure is treated as a conduct breach and reported to compliance
within 24 hours.

## 5.3 Pricing and structural mitigants {#5.3}

Approved exceptions carry a minimum pricing uplift of 50 basis points above
the standard risk-based price, a 20% limit reduction versus the applied
amount, and reviewable 12-month tenor with quarterly covenant testing.
Collateral top-up of at least 10 percentage points of loan-to-value applies
where the exception touches affordability or leverage ratios. Removal of
mitigants before the regularisation plan completes requires fresh committee
approval; automatic expiry of mitigants is prohibited.

## 5.4 Exception reporting and review {#5.4}

All exceptions are logged centrally with rule breached, mitigants, approver,
and expiry. The risk unit circulates a monthly exception dashboard; units
above a 7% exception rate must submit a remediation plan. Every exception
is re-tested at 6 months: cured exceptions return to standard terms,
uncured ones are exited or re-approved once only. Exception performance
(30+ days past due within 12 months of booking) feeds directly into the
approving officer's annual credit-quality scorecard.
