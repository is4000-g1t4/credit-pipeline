# Credit Documentation Requirements

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 4.1 Identity and KYC documents {#4.1}

Every application requires a valid national identity card or passport, proof
of residential address dated within 3 months, and completed beneficial
ownership declarations for entity borrowers. Employment-pass holders must
additionally provide the pass with at least 12 months validity and an
employer letter. Documents not in English must carry a certified translation.
Electronic KYC verification is acceptable only where the identity match
score exceeds 95%; below that, physical sighting of originals is mandatory
and must be attested by the witnessing officer.

## 4.2 Income and financial evidence {#4.2}

Salaried applicants provide 3 months of salary credits plus the latest Notice
of Assessment; self-employed applicants provide 2 years of assessments plus
6 months of bank statements. SME borrowers below S$1,000,000 exposure submit
12 months of operating-account statements and management accounts; above
S$1,000,000, audited financials for the last 2 years are mandatory. All bank
statements must be system-generated or bank-stamped; screenshots and edited
PDFs are rejected. Any document older than 3 months at decision date must be
refreshed, and stale documents are the most common cause of approval delays.

## 4.3 Collateral documentation and valuation {#4.3}

Property collateral requires a valuation report dated within 6 months from
an approved panel valuer, title search within 30 days, and fire insurance
assigned to the bank. Equipment and inventory collateral requires purchase
invoices, an independent valuation within 12 months, and quarterly stock
declarations for revolving charges. Receivables financing needs the aged
debtor listing under 90 days and assignment notices acknowledged by debtors
covering at least 120% of the facility. Any shortfall in insurance cover
suspends further drawdown until reinstated.

## 4.4 Execution, storage, and retention {#4.4}

Facility letters must be accepted within 30 days of issuance or they lapse
and require re-approval. All security documents are executed before first
disbursement with no exceptions; solicitor certification is required for
mortgages and debentures. Electronic records are retained for 7 years after
facility closure, physical title deeds in fire-rated custody with dual
access control. Missing-document reports are reviewed weekly, and any file
with critical documents outstanding beyond 14 days past disbursement is
escalated to the team manager with disbursement freeze authority.
