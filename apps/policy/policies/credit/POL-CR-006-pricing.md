# Risk-Based Pricing Standard

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 6.1 Pricing framework and floors {#6.1}

Every facility is priced as funding cost plus operating margin plus expected
loss plus capital charge, with an absolute floor of 3.0% per annum for
secured and 6.0% for unsecured lending. Pricing below the grid requires an
exception under POL-CR-005 and counts against the unit exception cap.
Relationship discounts are capped at 30 basis points and only for customers
with at least 3 active products and 24 months of clean conduct. Teaser or
step-up rates that fall below the floor in any period are prohibited.

## 6.2 Individual unsecured pricing grid {#6.2}

Unsecured individual pricing adds a risk margin of 300 to 800 basis points
over funding cost by bureau-score band: 650-699 pays the top of the range,
700-749 the middle, and 750+ the bottom. Tenure beyond 5 years adds
50 basis points; loan amounts above S$150,000 add 25 basis points. Staff
rates are fixed at funding cost plus 200 basis points with no further
discount. Early settlement within 12 months incurs a fee of 2% of the
outstanding balance, waived only for documented financial hardship.

## 6.3 SME and secured pricing grid {#6.3}

SME term facilities carry margins of 150 to 450 basis points over the base
rate by internal risk grade, with grades 1-3 at the bottom and grades 6-7
at the top; grades 8 and below are decline. Property-secured facilities earn
a 100 basis point collateral discount versus the unsecured equivalent, and
cash-secured facilities price at funding cost plus 100 basis points flat.
Fixed-rate SME loans include a 75 basis point term premium for tenures over
5 years. Floating-rate floors apply: the contractual rate never falls below
the floor even if the benchmark turns negative.

## 6.4 Repricing and margin review {#6.4}

Risk grades are refreshed annually and margins re-tested; downgrades of
2 or more grades trigger mandatory repricing of at least 100 basis points
or a limit reduction to restore the original risk-adjusted return. Missed
covenants add a 150 basis point default margin until 2 consecutive quarters
of compliance. Borrowers must be notified 30 days before any rate increase,
with a documented right to prepay without penalty inside that window.
Upward repricing above 200 basis points in 12 months needs committee sign-off.
