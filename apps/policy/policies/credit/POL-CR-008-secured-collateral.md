# Secured Lending and Collateral Standard

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 8.1 Loan-to-value caps {#8.1}

Maximum loan-to-value (LTV) is 75% for completed residential property, 60%
for commercial property, and 50% for industrial property and land. New
property launches under construction are capped 10 percentage points below
the completed-property equivalent. Equipment financing is capped at 70% of
invoice value for new equipment and 50% for used. Any LTV above these caps
requires exception approval under POL-CR-005 with mortgage insurance or
equivalent enhancement; LTV above 90% in any category is prohibited.

## 8.2 Valuation and revaluation {#8.2}

Initial valuations must come from the approved panel and be dated within
6 months of disbursement; desktop or indexed valuations are accepted only
for top-ups below S$100,000 on residential collateral. Commercial collateral
above S$2,000,000 requires 2 independent valuations with the lower adopted.
Revaluation is annual for commercial and industrial collateral and every
3 years for residential. A valuation drop exceeding 15% since origination
triggers a collateral review with potential limit reduction or top-up call
within 60 days.

## 8.3 Margin calls and top-up {#8.3}

Where current LTV exceeds the origination cap by 10 percentage points or
more, a margin call is issued requiring cure within 30 days via partial
repayment, additional collateral, or both. Failure to cure converts the
facility to reviewable tenor with monthly monitoring until compliance is
restored. For securities-backed facilities the trigger is tighter: a 5
percentage point breach forces same-week cure, and a 15 point breach permits
immediate partial liquidation. Waiver of a margin call is an exception and
consumes unit exception capacity under POL-CR-005.

## 8.4 Insurance and collateral maintenance {#8.4}

All property collateral carries fire insurance for full reinstatement value
assigned to the bank, renewed annually with proof filed 30 days before
expiry. Equipment collateral requires all-risk cover; inventory under
floating charge needs quarterly stock audits. Lapse of insurance suspends
further drawdowns and adds a 100 basis point uninsured margin until cover
is reinstated. Borrowers must notify the bank of any material damage,
compulsory acquisition notice, or tenancy change affecting more than 20% of
rental income within 7 days of occurrence.
