# Individual Unsecured Underwriting Standard

> Synthetic policy for evaluation only. Not issued by any real bank and not legal advice.

## 1.1 Eligibility criteria {#1.1}

Applicants must be aged 21 to 65 at application, and be Singapore citizens,
permanent residents, or employment-pass holders with at least 12 months
remaining validity. Minimum annual income is S$24,000 for salaried employees
and S$36,000 for self-employed applicants. The applicant must hold an active
salary-crediting or operating account of at least 6 months standing. Existing
customers with an internal delinquency record in the past 12 months are
ineligible for new unsecured facilities until all arrears are cleared for a
full 90-day seasoning period.

## 1.2 Credit bureau and score thresholds {#1.2}

A bureau report must be pulled within 14 days of decision. Standard approval
requires a bureau score of 650 or above with no current delinquency. Scores
between 580 and 649 may only proceed under the exceptions policy
(POL-CR-005) with a mandatory 25% limit reduction and dual sign-off. Scores
below 580 are an automatic decline. Applicants with more than 3 enquiries in
the past 90 days, any bankruptcy record within 5 years, or aggregate
unsecured balances exceeding 12x monthly income across all lenders must be
declined regardless of score.

## 1.3 Income assessment {#1.3}

Salaried income is verified against the latest 3 months of salary credits or
the latest Notice of Assessment. Variable components (bonus, commission,
overtime) are haircut by 30% and averaged over 12 months. Self-employed
income uses the lower of the last 2 years' assessed income, less a 20%
volatility haircut. Rental income is recognised at 70% of contracted rent
with a stamped tenancy agreement of at least 12 months. Cash allowances
without documentary proof are excluded entirely from qualifying income.

## 1.4 Approval authorities {#1.4}

Facilities up to S$50,000 may be approved by a single credit officer. Amounts
above S$50,000 up to S$200,000 require a team manager countersignature.
Anything above S$200,000, any staff application, or any politically exposed
person application must go to the credit committee with a minimum quorum of
3 voting members. Officers may never approve their own applications, those
of connected parties, or any case where they overrode a system decline
without committee endorsement.

## 1.5 Declines and re-application {#1.5}

Declined applicants must receive the reason code in writing within 7 business
days. A 90-day cooling-off period applies before re-application after any
decline, except where the decline was solely for incomplete documentation.
Applicants declined twice in 12 months are barred for a further 12 months.
All declines with bureau scores below 580 are final for 6 months and may not
be overturned by exception. Override of a decline reason requires fresh
documentary evidence, not officer judgement alone.
