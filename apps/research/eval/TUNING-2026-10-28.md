## 1. Context analyst — remove the preflight snapshot

Question: the context runner fetched every officer screen in
`collectOfficerContext` and injected a 60k-char snapshot into the prompt; the
model then re-called many of the same tools. Does the model need the snapshot?

| Variant | pass@1 | pass^3 | Narrative drops/run | Tools/run | Cost | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Preflight on (baseline) | 1.000 | 30/30 | 0.83 (75) | 23.6 | $1.300 | 12.2s |
| Preflight off | 1.000 | 30/30 | **0.29 (26)** | **12.8** | **$1.089** | 12.4s |

30 cases × 3 trials, Terra, prompt `context-v5`, temp 0.1.

**Decision:** preflight is now **off by default**. The model starts with
`get_cashflow_metrics`, then opens the screens it needs. Accuracy is identical;
drops, tool calls, and cost fall. The old path remains available with
`CONTEXT_AGENT_PREFLIGHT_ENABLED=true` as a fallback for a weaker model.
The candidate coverage validator still enforces every deterministic candidate.

Raw: `context-models-2026-10-28T05-38-14-963Z.json` (on),
`context-models-2026-10-28T12-14-08-032Z.json` (off).

## 2. Case brief — remove the mandatory preflight searches

Question: the brief runner pre-fetched `adverse_media` and `registry` and
injected `<preflight_sources>`; can the model drive the searches?

| Variant | pass@1 | pass^3 | Entity match | Tools/run | Cost | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Preflight on (baseline) | 1.000 | 3/3 | **0.833** | 0.0 | **$0.177** | **18.2s** |
| Preflight off | 1.000 | 3/3 | 0.722 | 2.0 | $0.273 | 22.5s |

3 fixture cases × 3 trials, Sol, prompt `v6`, judge Terra.

**Decision:** keep the preflight. Code gates still pass, but entity grounding
drops and cost/latency rise because the model must spend turns searching.
This is a three-case directional result, not a 30-case claim.

Raw: `brief-2026-10-28T05-42-44-348Z.json` (on),
`brief-2026-10-28T12-08-55-110Z.json` (off).
